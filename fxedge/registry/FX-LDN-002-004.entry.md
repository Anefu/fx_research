## Results (pooled across 6 pairs; n=5,148 compressed / 10,470 non-compressed)

**FX-LDN-002/003 — ER uplift: PASS (relative effect is real and large)**
- ER median: compressed **0.82** vs non-compressed **0.55** (MW p < 1e-300; block-bootstrap diff CI [0.29, 0.36])
- P(ER>1): compressed **32.9%** vs non **10.8%** (z=33.7)
- P(ER>1.5): compressed **10.8%** vs non **2.4%**

**FX-LDN-004 — absolute movement: FAIL (the honest arbiter, decisive)**
- \|R_60m\|: compressed **3.59 bp** vs non **4.76 bp** — compressed sessions move LESS
- \|R_180m\|: compressed **7.95 bp** vs non **9.79 bp**
- Sign agreement: **0/6 pairs** in favor of uplift (all 6 negative)
- Year stability: deficit negative in **10/10 years** (−0.26 … −1.69 bp)
- Pre-registered DST split: deficit in both winter and summer clocks

**Diagnostic (labeled at gate time):** London range among compressed sessions
sits at the **33rd percentile** of its own 60-session rolling distribution
(vs 57th for non-compressed). The ER "expansion" is a denominator artifact:
quiet Asia shrinks the base, ratios go up, nothing actually moves more.

## Interpretation

- The relative observation is genuine and decade-stable but **not tradable as
  positioned**: you cannot eat a ratio. No entry, no stop, no target can be
  derived from ER inflation.
- Microstructural reading: quiet Asian sessions are followed by **ordinary or
  quieter** London sessions — calm feeds persist across the handover in
  absolute terms; only the denominator changes.
- Entry-construction experiments (registry §5/§6 ladder B5–B7) are **not run** —
  Gate L1 explicitly blocks building sweep/CISD/FVG layers on top of a
  mechanical effect.
